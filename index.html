Our Story

Our Story

We started as neighbours in same off campus apartment while we were still undergraduate students at the University of Portharcourt around 2017.

As times past, we began to exchange greetings and gradually we began to say a few words to each other and converse in general as we do with other neighbours especially during weekends or in our free-time because as a pharmacy student, she barely had free time so you could hardly see her around or speak with her that much. God so kind, after months, we got acquainted to each other and we became mutuals, gradually became friends. A friendship, I could describe as one that has been amazing and I am grateful for.

The friendship remained so until I graduated and left for my youth service in 2019. We kept in touch despite the distance and continued to support each other in everything and by every means possible by the help of God. One amazing thing about this journey is how far we had come and the years of friendship we have experienced.

In the later periods of 2020, I had a feeling of uncertainty if our friendship would be as solid as it has always been and this is because as at the time, I was preparing to leave Nigeria to further my studies and possibly, start a new life. I was concerned because I thought a new degree of distance might change everything but by the grace of God, we kept up as friends, we kept communicating even though it was becoming too demanding as our time schedules were different but it still didn't deter us from keeping up with each other and trusting God to help us. As a matter of fact, her support, prayers and words of encouragement helped me to navigate through the challenging periods I experienced in my early days away from home (Nigeria).

At the end of the day, love and patience won.

We are grateful for God's faithfulness and look forward to beginning our forever together, with His blessings and guidance.
